THE PAID CALL · GW-2 · GW-3

One order. Every paid call runs it.

The gateway never improvises the sequence: a payment is checked, then policed, then the seller is called, and only then does money move.
01 VERIFY Delegate signature, access-key nonce and balance — read from NEAR, not from the request body. chain read
02 GUARDS Replay key, one payment in flight per payer, expiry window against the upstream timeout, blocklist. before any call
03 UPSTREAM The seller's GET, dialled once, with its own deadline and a loopback refusal — GET, and nothing else. GET only
04 SETTLE The one gate on settlement. A 3xx, 4xx, 5xx or timeout abandons the row and charges nobody. 2xx or nothing
